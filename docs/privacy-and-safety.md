# Privacy & Safety — Coexistence Console

## What Data Is Stored

| Data | Purpose | Storage | Retention |
|------|---------|---------|-----------|
| postId | Identify posts in queue | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| subredditId | Scope policies and analytics | Redis | Duration of installation |
| title/body preview | Display in review queue | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| signal flags | Show why post needs review | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| mod action + note | Record moderator decision | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| ActionLog rows | Generate transparent moderation analytics | Redis | 90 days TTL |
| timestamps | Sort queue, calculate analytics | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| hashed authorId | Privacy-preserving attribution | Redis | 30 days or until post deleted |
| aggregate fallback metrics | Dashboard time-saved fallback stats | Redis | 30 days TTL |
| optional policy drafts | Moderator-reviewed policy copy generated by AI Policy Copilot | Redis | Duration of installation |
| optional pulse summaries | Aggregate Community Pulse summaries generated from ActionLog counts and queue reasons | UI state only | Not persisted by the app |

## What Data Is NOT Stored

- Full user profiles or histories
- Personal attributes (political, religious, health, etc.)
- Raw comment data (only post previews)
- Data for machine learning or model training
- Data sold or shared with third parties
- Post content sent to an LLM for classification or scoring

## Deletion & TTL

- **PostDelete trigger**: Immediately removes the review item and keys for that post.
- **CommentDelete trigger**: Acknowledged (app does not store comment data).
- **Redis expire**: Review, recent-snippet, ActionLog, activity, and fallback metrics keys use TTLs for automatic cleanup.
- **Policy data**: Stored in Redis under the installation's subreddit scope.

## Safety Principles

1. **Signal, not accusation**: We never say "this is AI." We say "this has signals that match your community's review criteria."
2. **Moderator decides**: The app organizes information. The human moderator makes the final call.
3. **Default is AI-friendly**: Out-of-the-box settings avoid automatic punishment and keep useful AI-assisted contributions moving.
4. **Opt-in for strict**: Auto-removal and strict policies require explicit moderator configuration.
5. **LLM is moderator-assist only**: Verified Gemini generation drafts moderator policy text and aggregate Community Pulse summaries only. It is never used for post scoring, user profiling, or enforcement decisions.
6. **Provider boundary**: Fireworks may be used for local development smoke tests, but the live Devvit app keeps AI calls on Reddit-approved provider domains.
